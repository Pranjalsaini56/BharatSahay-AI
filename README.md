# 🇮🇳 BharatSahay AI

### AI-Powered Public Information Assistant

BharatSahay AI is a concept for an AI-powered public information assistance platform designed to help Indian citizens discover, understand, and access information relevant to their needs.

The project aims to reduce information inequality by making public information easier to find, understand, and act upon.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Despite the availability of government schemes, educational opportunities, healthcare services, and other public resources, many citizens struggle to identify information relevant to their personal circumstances.

Information is often scattered across multiple platforms and presented in complex language, making it difficult for people to understand their options and take appropriate action.

## 💡 Our Solution

BharatSahay AI proposes a context-aware public information assistant that simplifies access to relevant information through an easy-to-use interface.

Users can select their life stage, explore relevant public services, understand simplified information, and receive guidance on eligibility, required documents, and application procedures.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Life-Stage Based Access:** Organizes information according to user needs, such as students, workers, homemakers, and senior citizens.
- **Public Service Discovery:** Helps users explore relevant government schemes and public services.
- **Simplified Information:** Presents complex information in an easy-to-understand format.
- **Eligibility Assistance:** Supports users in understanding eligibility requirements.
- **Actionable Guidance:** Provides information about required documents and application steps.
- **Context-Aware Assistance:** Aims to make information more relevant to individual circumstances.
- **Multilingual Accessibility:** Designed with the goal of improving access across language barriers.

## 🏗️ Proposed System Architecture

The proposed architecture follows a layered approach:

1. **User Interface:** Web browser through which citizens interact with the platform.
2. **Frontend:** HTML and CSS-based user interface.
3. **Backend:** Python and Flask-based server for handling requests.
4. **AI Processing Layer:** NLP processing, text summarization, and context analysis.
5. **Knowledge Base:** Curated public information used to provide relevant guidance.

## 🌍 Target Users

- Students looking for educational opportunities and scholarships.
- Workers seeking relevant public services and welfare information.
- Homemakers looking for applicable schemes and resources.
- Senior citizens seeking accessible public information.
- Citizens who need simplified guidance to navigate public services.

## 🚀 Vision

To make public information more accessible, understandable, and actionable for every citizen, regardless of their location, background, or familiarity with digital platforms.
